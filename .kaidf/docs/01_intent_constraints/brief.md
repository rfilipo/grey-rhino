# Intent

Relaunch the Grey Rhino server as a governed, AI-assisted operation: a weekly
editorial cycle planned as a contract, content drafted by a local model inside a
prepared environment, every post decided by a human, and the whole operation
auditable in this repository. The case doubles as public evidence that K-AIDF
governs operations, not only software.

# Constraints

- **Member approval gate.** The server is suspended (2026-11-18) until its members
  formally approve the relaunch. No execution, no publishing, before that gate.
- **Yuno's privacy.** The chief analyst never appears — no live, video or voice
  obligation, in any format, ever.
- **Regulatory red line.** No investment recommendations, no signals, no promises
  of return. Education and scenarios only (CVM/SEC).
- **Human gate on everything published.** Automation drafts and routes; humans approve.
- **Platform limits.** Discord posts stay under 2,000 characters; the team never
  DMs members with offers (anti-scam rule).
- **Small is fine.** Five members today. Quality and trust over growth at any cost.
- **Known dependency.** The n8n TLS certificate expired 2026-11-13; the automation
  leg waits on its renewal (team) — the operation must also work by hand.

# Success criteria

1. Member approval formalized and recorded in this repository.
2. Roles letter (Ricardo x Yuno) agreed and stored.
3. One full dry-run week completed inside the approval queue (nothing published).
4. First governed week published under the cycle contract.
5. First `kob audit` of this repository recorded in the verification document,
   with a monthly audit + scorecard ritual established.
